TAMIL-AI.ME: Sangam Poetry Finally Gets a Search Bar

The problem. Tamil classical literature is hard to search and understand with today's AI tools.

What I built. TAMIL-AI.ME, a FastAPI and ChromaDB backend with a React frontend. IndicTrans2 translates between Tamil and English, multilingual-e5 provides the embeddings, and an OCR step (the KalVettu module) reads printed Tamil with Tesseract. It does not read stone inscriptions yet.

4,500+ entries across 22 sources indexed

Next. Moving from local development to a cloud deployment, to learn MLOps. Some entries still lack a translation or commentary.

AIRES: Blocked Road? The Ambulance Re-routes Itself

The problem. Ambulances lose minutes when roads get blocked mid-trip.

What I built. AIRES, an AI emergency-response MVP with live re-routing on a real Trichy road map. It has driver, hospital and control-panel dashboards, and events are injected through the control panel.

7.77 → 5.86 min Simulation result: across 300 simulated runs, trip time fell about 25%, and AIRES was never worse.

Next. Replace the simulated events and placeholder hospital data with real inputs.

CULLINAN AI: Teaching an Image Model One Face

The problem. Personalised AI images often give a generic face that only loosely resembles the person.

What I built. CULLINAN AI, a personalised image-generation project. So far: a face recognition and verification pipeline (InsightFace and ArcFace), and a Stable Diffusion 1.5 and Kohya_ss setup ready for identity-specific LoRA training.

10/10 · 7/7 genuine faces accepted · impostors rejected, on my own evaluation set (69 reference images). Not proof of universal accuracy.

Next. Train the identity LoRA, then check generated faces against the original identity. The image generator itself is not finished yet.

Eye-Care-Tool: 20-20-20, Now With a Webcam

The problem. Long screen sessions cause digital eye strain, and a fixed reminder timer is easy to ignore.

What I built. Eye-Care-Tool applies the 20-20-20 rule but checks whether you are really active. It adds webcam fatigue detection, Tamil notifications, Telegram push and SQLite stats.

What the research says

For. In a study of 29 symptomatic computer users at Aston University (Talens-Estarelles et al., Contact Lens & Anterior Eye, 2023), webcam-based 20-20-20 reminders reduced eye strain and dry-eye symptoms. The improvement did not last a week after the reminders stopped.

Against. A 30-person test (Johnson & Rosenfield, Optometry and Vision Science, 2023) found that 20-second breaks did not reduce symptoms in a 40-minute tablet task.

My take. Inspired by the 20-20-20 rule. Research is mixed, so this is a reminder aid, not a medical treatment.
